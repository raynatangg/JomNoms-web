# JomNoms

**Can't decide what to eat? JomNoms picks for you.**

[Open the app →](https://raynatangg.github.io/JomNoms-web/)

---

Everyone knows the feeling. You're hungry, your friends are hungry, and the
only answer anyone can give is "anything lah". Twenty minutes later you're
still standing there, still hungry, still deciding.

JomNoms ends that. Tell it how you feel, and it picks a real place near you.

## How it works

1. **Share your location** — or type in where you are.
2. **Pick a mood** — not a cuisine. Nobody knows what cuisine they want.
3. **Get an answer** — one real restaurant, with directions.

Don't like the pick? Ask again. It keeps a shortlist of the other options too,
so you can see what else was close by.

## Moods, not menus

Six moods, because "what do you want to eat" is the wrong question:

- **Quick Bite** — no time, just feed me
- **Healthy** — being good today
- **Comfort Food** — had a day
- **Cafe Vibes** — somewhere to sit and talk
- **Sweet Tooth** — dessert is a meal
- **Fine Dining** — occasion food

## Speaks your language

English, Bahasa Melayu, 简体中文, and 日本語. Switch anytime from the header.

## Made for Malaysia

Built around how people here actually eat — mamak, kopitiam, hawker stalls,
and everything in between, not just the places with a marketing budget.

## No friction

- **No download.** It's a web page. Open it and it works.
- **No account.** No sign-up, no email, no password.
- **No app store.** Works the same on your phone, tablet, and laptop.
- **Free.** No ads, no subscription, nothing to pay.

## Once you have your answer

Tap through to Waze or Google Maps and go eat.
